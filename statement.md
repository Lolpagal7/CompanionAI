# Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Its core mission is to address social isolation, anxiety, and depression using artificial intelligence as a companion capable of emotional intelligence

## Scope

To develop and deploy a multimodal empathetic chatbot system that identifies and responds to human emotions to help address social isolation, anxiety, and depression.

## High-level Features

- Multimodal Emotion Recognition: The system analyzes user emotions simultaneously through text input using a fine-tuned RoBERTa-base model and visual facial cues processed by Convolutional Neural Networks.  
- Empathetic Conversational Engine: It generates context-aware, empathetic responses that are directly modulated by the user's detected emotional state using the BlenderBot model.


## Target User
 - Depressed and isolated students, faculty and office workers
 - People Suffering from social anxiety, anxious attacks and depression
 - People going through hardships in life
